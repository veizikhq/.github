# Security

Report a suspected vulnerability by email: **security@veizik.com**

Please do not open a public issue or discussion for anything with a security consequence — including ways around activation or licensing. If you are unsure whether something is a security matter, email us; a misrouted report is better than a public one.

Useful to include: the release tag you installed and the output of `veizik doctor`, what you did, what you observed and what you expected.

How Veizik handles your machine and your data: https://veizik.com/security
